Defend the Village!

Thornhallow, in Hallowshire. The dead walk down from Ashhollow Castle every night, and Robert Bailiff has bolted his door. That leaves you.

Your colour

A guide, the controls (you can change them) and options. Esc opens it in the game.

Build 3: the inn and the ruins

  • The Thorny Rose: bring food by day, drink by night, burst out full of Dutch courage.
  • Ruins to search, inside the wall and out. The outer ones change every night. Relics turn up, more often by moonlight.
  • Every weapon has a trick on Shift. Seven found weapons; a bow and a crossbow shoot over the wall.
  • A pack with slots (I), a quick weapon swap (X), and an arms rack in the storehouse for spares.
  • A handbook on Esc: a guide, controls you can change, and options.
  • Ten books, seven ranks each.
  • The priest blesses things and teaches holy studies.
  • Posses have nerve, take orders (Q) and emergency toilet breaks (T).

Changed after play

  • The dead rise all along the graveyard, in bigger, tougher hordes. No more waves: they come all night without a pause, faster as it goes on.
  • Stone, iron and fishing move every morning. Smaller outcrop and mine.
  • Stumps rot into saplings, which grow back into trees.
  • Old barricades rot by half each evening unless braced with iron.
  • A smaller keep, with nobody starting the day inside it. It turns see-through when something is behind it.
  • The map’s edge is a thorn hedge, a line of stakes and the river.
  • Taller flags, smaller name tags.
  • Notices are scrolls now, sealed with wax.
Build 2: the first week
  • Seven nights, ending with the Steward. Stone, iron, food and fishing.
  • The market, the storehouse, the smithy and the slum.
  • Repairs and reinforcing. Bodies as walls and decoys.
  • Relatives, cowardice, and saving every morning.
Build 1: the first night
  • The map of Thornhallow, from Hallowshire Forest to Ashhollow Castle.
  • Chop wood, build on the north foundations, place barricades and spike rows.
  • Rally neighbours into a posse. Shamblers and skeletons.
  • Co-op for up to 8, by village code.

Still to come: merchants, weather, hired guards and mercenaries, contraptions, and nights 8 to 30. Saves from Build 2 do not carry over.
